# DoS Attack
A DoS (Denial-of-Service) attack is a cyber-attack that makes a computer, network, or service unavailable to its legitimate users by flooding it with overwhelming amounts of illegitimate traffic or requests, thus consuming its resources like bandwidth, CPU, or memory. The goal is to overload the targeted system to the point where it cannot process legitimate requests, causing it to slow down or crash completely.

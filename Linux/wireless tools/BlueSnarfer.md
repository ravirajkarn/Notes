# Bluesnarfing

**Bluesnarfing** is the [unauthorized access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Access_control "Access control") of information from a [wireless device](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wireless_device "Wireless device") through a [Bluetooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluetooth "Bluetooth") connection, often between phones, desktops, laptops, and PDAs ([personal digital assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Personal_digital_assistant "Personal digital assistant")).[1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bluesnarfing) This allows access to calendars, contact lists, emails and text messages, and on some phones, users can copy pictures and private videos. Bluesnarfing works by tricking devices into pairing with each other so that they can exchange information without anyone knowing about it.

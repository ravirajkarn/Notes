# Big O Notation
## 1. Big O Notation
**O()** It represent the **upper Bound**. Means how fast our operation performers. bast case

## 2. Big $\Omega$ Notation
**$\Omega$ (Omega)** It represent the **Lower Bound**. Means minimum amount of time required for any operation. Operation will at least a certain amount of time. worst case.

## 3. Big $\Theta$ Notation

**$\Theta$ (Theta)**  Notation combines **Upper bounds with lower bounds to get tight bounds**. average case.

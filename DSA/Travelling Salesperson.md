In this case there is no proper proper algorithm to calculate shortest distance between paths. **Worst Case is O(!n)**. 
his is one of the unsolved problems in computer science.
here’s no fast known algorithm for it, and smart people think it’s
impossible to have a smart algorithm for this problem.
